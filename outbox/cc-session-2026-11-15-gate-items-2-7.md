## Item 4: `scripts/run-steps.mjs` counts the skips behind the failing step

`stepSummary` counted every skipped step as "behind" the failing one. With the Chromium gate's final step the failing step is the LAST one, so the twenty browser checks that did not run were reported as "did not run behind it" when they ran (were skipped) before it. Now `skipped` counts only the steps after the first failing step, in the job's own order, `skippedBefore` counts those before it, and the line says both (`Nothing was skipped behind it ... 20 steps before it were skipped by their own if`). `scripts/status.mjs`'s clause carries the before-count too. Consequences on the real tree, all measured: the `verify` job still reads 20 browser steps and 77 browser-free ones; `verify-full.yml`'s Confidence gate is now read as a browser step (it spawns the gate's rows), which is what item 6 needed from item 2, and the mutation run inside the shard action is read as one too; the two annotations that said otherwise were corrected (`N` 2 to 1 for `verify-full.yml`, 3 to 2 for the shard action). Self-test 279 of 279; `python3 scripts/check-browser-gate.py --plain-commands` prints the 67 commands whose browser-freeness is a claim.
