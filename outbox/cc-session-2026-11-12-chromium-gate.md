# Session note: the verify job's browser steps wait for the Chromium install, and nothing else does (2026-11-12)

**Branch:** `option1-chromium-gate`, stacked on `e1-citations-head` (`2ff620c`). **Owning CC-CMD:** `docs/CC-CMD-2026-11-11-runner-apt-mirror-dns.md`, option 1 ("isolate the failure"), the part of that CC-CMD the owner did not choose on 2026-11-11 and then asked to move toward completing on 2026-11-12 ("while moving towards completion of root of the problem"). **Scope:** the `verify` job of `.github/workflows/verify.yml` only. The `full` job of `verify-full.yml` and the composite mutation-shard action also install a browser and are not changed.

## What was wrong

On 2026-11-11 `Install Chromium` failed (the runner could not resolve the Ubuntu mirrors for about three hours; the cause is not established, the CC-CMD lists it as not known) and GitHub skipped every later step of the `verify` job that had no `if`: 63 steps, of which 17 needed a browser, 44 did not (the F# suite among them) and 2 were the setup actions' post-job cleanups (measured by running this guard's classification over that run's tree, `7e1a7e5`, and cross-checked against the independent table: 0 disagreements). This note first said "about sixty checks that never needed a browser"; that was wrong, see the review section. The commit was reported unverified for a reason that said nothing about the commit. Options 2 and 3 (no apt, a three-minute limit) shrank the exposure; this is the structural half.

## What was built

- `verify.yml`: `Install Chromium` gains `id: chromium` and `continue-on-error: true`; the 20 steps that need a browser carry `if: steps.chromium.outcome == 'success'` (the one in the tail, "Render check against the LIVE slate", extends its existing `if`); a final step, `always()` and `steps.chromium.outcome != 'success'`, fails the job, so it is never green over skipped steps. It tests `!= 'success'` and not `== 'failure'`: a step killed by `timeout-minutes` may conclude as something other than `failure`. A new job `browser-gate` runs the guard. After the review (below): the final step takes the outcome through `env: INSTALL_OUTCOME` and prints one of three messages (the install was skipped because an earlier step failed first; the run was cancelled; the install did not succeed); the self-tests of six render checks (`check:viewport-overflow`, `check:axis`, `check:draw`, `check:verify-panel`, `check:metrics`, `check:team-tints`), which launch no browser, are one plain ungated step directly after the install; and `continue-on-error` is on the install and nowhere else in the job.
- `scripts/check-browser-gate.py` (Python and PyYAML, the repo's precedent for parsing workflow YAML): classifies each step from the scripts it runs and checks the eight rules in its header. As built at `3528bb5` it had five (id and `continue-on-error`; every browser step gated; no browser-free step gated; the exact final step; every `steps.<id>` an `if` names exists) and read a command it did not parse as browser-free. As fixed (`6ea67d9`): it RUNS the final step's script under `bash -e -o pipefail` for each outcome the install can have and requires a non-zero exit and an `::error::` line, requires that step to be the LAST one, refuses `continue-on-error` anywhere else and a bare `if: false`, allows only `always()`, `success()`, `!cancelled()` and `steps.<id>.outcome == 'success'` next to the gate, and READS EVERY STEP AFTER THE INSTALL FAIL CLOSED (a shell tokenizer; a wrapper, a heredoc, inline `node -e`, a missing or cyclic npm script, a local composite action is refused, not called browser-free). `npm run check:browser-gate` runs it.

## How a step is classified, and the measurement behind it

By the entry script the step starts (`node <entry>`, directly or through `npm run`), flag-aware: a `--self-test` invocation never launches, and an imported module never runs (import-purity). A mechanical read that followed imports called 9 steps browser steps that independent readers did not.

**Independent verification (Rule 89):** `outbox/verify-step-browser-classification-2026-11-12.json`. Workflow `verify-step-browser-classification`: two verifiers per slice of 8 of the 90 steps after the install, who read the sources and were told not to read the mechanical table; 86 agreed, 4 went to a judge. 20 steps need Chromium, 70 do not. No browser-free step depends on the output of an earlier step other than `Build`. The guard agrees with the independent verdict on 90 of 90; the import-level read disagreed on 8.
