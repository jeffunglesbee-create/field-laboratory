## What it is

A new tab after Scout's Pick. Each window of the day (ET; every day 12pm, 2pm, 4pm, 7:30pm, 10pm; weekends also 7:30am and 10am; each an hour either side) shows Scout's choice among the games that kick off in it, by Scout's rules unchanged (`chooseScoutAt`). Every window is listed, empty or not. Edge kickoffs follow the owner's rulings; a kickoff outside every window joins the nearest, halfway to the later. A game belongs only to the window it kicks off in. Games that cannot be placed are counted by reason at the foot: no kickoff time, not a two-sided contest, or a kickoff on another day than its slate's.

Golf is out by those rules with no special case: its round rows carry no kickoff time today, and it is not a two-sided contest when it will (field-relay-nba `docs/CC-CMD-2026-11-16-golf-tee-times.md`). `PGA Tour` rows never reach the board: the decoder counts a row with no sides as unnamed at the desk.
