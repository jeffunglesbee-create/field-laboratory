## Design

The live fact lives in ESPN, reached through `/v2/games`. The archive (`/context/date`, cached 300 s, written final-gated by a `*/15` tick) is the wrong channel for a live card, and a relay write of an in-play score into it would stamp `finalized_at` and render `Final` mid-match. So:

- **The lab joins `/v2/games` onto the row it already has**, by `EspnEventId`, in two pure functions: `LiveFeed.readingFor` (the join by ESPN event id and the decode of one game into a `LiveReading`) and `LiveScore.foldReading` (the merge of a reading into a card's `GameState`). A reading carries a score only for a feed state `live` or `final`, never from `pre`, and **never sets `FinalizedAt`**. (The first draft of this document named one function, `applyLive`, that was never built under that name.) `gameStateAt` is unchanged and no fourth `GameState` case is added. *(As built: the join is the poll's, by ESPN event id; the merge is `LiveScore.foldReading`, folded inside the card over its own state signal. Pick'em and Stats read the archive row alone and do not see a reading, so for the minutes a match is in play the card says `In progress` where the Pick'em chip says `PENDING`; this paragraph first said the three "keep agreeing", which was the intent and not what was built, found by review 2026-11-11. The parity is `docs/CC-CMD-2026-11-11-live-scores-tabs.md`.)*
- **Full time:** feed `final` with no `finalized_at` on the row, and `Final` not yet true, shows `In progress` with a `Full time` marker. `Final` arrives only from the archive.
- **A started row with no served score** reads a named status, never `Scheduled <iso>`. Built as `Start time passed, no score served` (CC's wording; the owner may change it). It says what is known: a postponed fixture is also a row whose start time has passed and that has no score, so `Kicked off` would be a claim the row does not support. Pre-kickoff text is unchanged byte for byte.
- **If the feed fails or has no match for the card:** a named state (proposed `live score unavailable`), never `—` and `Scheduled`. No fallback to a capture.
- **Stats:** `Stats.liveMargin` has no sport gate, so once a soccer row is `InProgress` the Stats tab would print baseball wording (`N-run gap`, `needs an inning`). It is gated to non-soccer first (Task 3).
- **The poll, as conditions (CC's proposal, for the owner to change):** `LIVE_POLL_MS = 60000`; a card is *in window* from its kickoff to kickoff + 3 h; the poll runs only while at least one soccer card is in window and `document.visibilityState === 'visible'`, and stops otherwise; one `GET /v2/games?sport=<key>&date=<ET field day>` per lab soccer key that has a card in window per tick (one key on the EPL matchday), none for a key whose previous request is still open, and a request unanswered after `LivePollTimeoutMs` (90 s, one and a half periods, added by review 2026-11-11 poll S4 and S18; a bound, not a measurement of the relay) is cut so its key is asked again and the card says the feed did not answer. Arithmetic (Rule 78, corrected 2026-11-11): a viewer asks once per key per 60 s from the first card's kickoff to the last card's kickoff + 3 h of that key's day, the UNION of that day's windows, not 3 h (a key with one 19:00Z kickoff is 180 requests; for illustration only, not the 2026-11-21 slate, kickoffs from 12:30Z to 17:30Z make an 8 h union and 480 requests). Each request costs the relay what fact 6 lists: one BSD call regardless of liveness, three ESPN fetches per second-leg game, one `/wp` write per live game, and the ESPN scoreboard fetch is edge-cached for 15 s. `AmbientDO` makes up to 720 requests per key per 3 h while a game is live (every 15 s), so ONE viewer for ONE 3 h match adds 25% to that key's `/v2/games` request count and ten add 250%; over a longer union window the ratio is the same per hour while `AmbientDO` is polling at its live rate and larger while it is idle at 60 s. That is the reason for 60 s and for the visibility gate. A relay-side `cf` cache on the BSD call would cut it and is relay-directed (Task 11).
- **Reader refresh remains:** the Today button re-runs `start()`.

## Open questions for the owner (found by review `wf_584a08a5-824`, 2026-11-11; each is a design choice, none decided here)

1. **A reading has no age (poll S3 and S12).** The poll stops at kickoff + 3 h (your ruling's window, built as `LiveScore.liveWindowAfterStart`), and a hidden tab makes no request. A card keeps its last answer, `1–0`, `In progress`, `67'`, until the page is reloaded or the archive finalises, with nothing on the card saying how old that answer is. Real cases: a cup tie that runs to extra time and a shootout can pass 3 h from kickoff (the review estimated about 2 h 45 min or more; not measured), and a suspended match (lightning, routine in MLS) is still `In progress`. Options: (a) leave it, the window is yours; (b) extend the window for knockout competitions; (c) write the instant each answer was heard into the reading and have the card say `as of HH:mm` once it is older than two polls, and a named state when the window ends. (c) is the review's proposal and touches `LiveReading`, `liveNoteFor` and the render check; it has not been built.
2. **A re-render asks again at once (poll S0).** The Today button, the date buttons and Back re-run `start()`, which redraws the cards and starts a new poll whose first tick is immediate. A reader who presses Today every 20 s makes 3 requests per key per minute, and each new poll has its own one-request-per-key guard. Both skeptics rated it low. It is what makes a re-drawn card show its reading right away rather than a minute later. Options: (a) leave it, as built; (b) keep the last asked instant per key and day outside `startLivePoll`, and re-apply the last reading to the new cards. The conditions above say "one request per key per tick"; as built that holds inside one poll, and a re-render starts a new one.
