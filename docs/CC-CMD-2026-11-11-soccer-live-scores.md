## Design

The live fact lives in ESPN, reached through `/v2/games`. The archive (`/context/date`, cached 300 s, written final-gated by a `*/15` tick) is the wrong channel for a live card, and a relay write of an in-play score into it would stamp `finalized_at` and render `Final` mid-match. So:

- **The lab joins `/v2/games` onto the row it already has**, by `EspnEventId`, in two pure functions: `LiveFeed.readingFor` (the join by ESPN event id and the decode of one game into a `LiveReading`) and `LiveScore.foldReading` (the merge of a reading into a card's `GameState`). A reading carries a score only for a feed state `live` or `final`, never from `pre`, and **never sets `FinalizedAt`**. (The first draft of this document named one function, `applyLive`, that was never built under that name.) `gameStateAt` is unchanged and no fourth `GameState` case is added. *(As built: the poll hands the decoded feed to `LiveFeed.readingFor`, which does the join by ESPN event id; the merge is `LiveScore.foldReading`, folded inside the card over its own state signal. Pick'em and Stats read the archive row alone and do not see a reading, so for the minutes a match is in play the card says `In progress` where the Pick'em chip says `PENDING`; this paragraph first said the three "keep agreeing", which was the intent and not what was built, found by review 2026-11-11. The parity is `docs/CC-CMD-2026-11-11-live-scores-tabs.md`.)*
- **Half time:** feed `live` with `situation.isHalftime` true and both scores shows `In progress` with a `Half time` note (`LiveScore.HalfTime`, `LiveFeed.readingFor`); the flag is read from a live game only (a `pre` or `final` game that carried it is still no reading, or full time), a flag that is not a boolean fails the feed by name like a string score, and a live game at half time with no score is unavailable, never `0–0`.
- **Full time:** feed `final` with no `finalized_at` on the row, and `Final` not yet true, shows `In progress` with a `Full time` marker. `Final` arrives only from the archive.
- **A started row with no served score** reads a named status, never `Scheduled <iso>`. Built as `Start time passed, no score served` (CC's wording; the owner may change it). It says what is known: a postponed fixture is also a row whose start time has passed and that has no score, so `Kicked off` would be a claim the row does not support. Pre-kickoff text is unchanged byte for byte.
- **If the feed fails or has no match for the card:** a named state (proposed `live score unavailable`), never `—` and `Scheduled`. No fallback to a capture.
- **Stats:** `Stats.liveMargin` has no sport gate, so once a soccer row is `InProgress` the Stats tab would print baseball wording (`N-run gap`, `needs an inning`). It is gated to non-soccer first (Task 3).
- **The poll, as conditions (CC's proposal, for the owner to change):** `LIVE_POLL_MS = 60000`; a card is *in window* from its kickoff to kickoff + 3 h; the poll runs only while at least one soccer card is in window and `document.visibilityState === 'visible'`, and stops otherwise; one `GET /v2/games?sport=<key>&date=<ET field day>` per lab soccer key that has a card in window per tick (one key on the EPL matchday), none for a key whose previous request is still open, and a request unanswered after `LivePollTimeoutMs` (90 s, one and a half periods, added by review 2026-11-11 poll S4 and S18; a bound, not a measurement of the relay) is cut so its key is asked again, and the card then reads `live score unavailable` with `the feed did not answer` as its `data-why`. The one-request-per-key guard is per poll: a re-render (Today, the date buttons, Back) starts a new poll with its own guard (open question 2). Arithmetic (Rule 78, corrected 2026-11-11): a viewer asks once per key per 60 s from the first card's kickoff to the last card's kickoff + 3 h of that key's day, the UNION of that day's windows, not 3 h (a key with one 19:00Z kickoff is 180 requests; for illustration only, not the 2026-11-21 slate, kickoffs from 12:30Z to 17:30Z make an 8 h union and 480 requests). Each request costs the relay what fact 6 lists: one BSD call regardless of liveness, three ESPN fetches per second-leg game, one `/wp` write per live game, and the ESPN scoreboard fetch is edge-cached for 15 s. `AmbientDO` polls `/v2/games` only for `epl, mls, laliga, seriea, bundesliga, ligue1` in November (`wc26` only in months 5 to 7, January being 0, so June to August; relay `src/ambient-do.js` `AMBIENT_SPORTS` and `SPORT_ACTIVE_MONTHS`, read at `9f666e05` and again at `76edcbab`, 2026-11-11). For those six keys it makes up to 720 requests per key per 3 h while a game is live (every 15 s), so ONE viewer for ONE 3 h match adds at most 25% to that key's `/v2/games` request count and ten add 250%; over a longer union window the ratio is the same per hour while `AmbientDO` is polling at its live rate and larger while it is idle at 60 s. Its self-call carries `cf: { cacheTtl: 25 }` (`ambient-do.js` line 1210 of that copy), which may lower that baseline and so raise the share; its effect is not measured here, and the sibling `docs/CC-CMD-2026-11-11-v2-games-bsd-cache.md` says the same. For the other ten lab soccer keys (`ucl`, `uclqual`, `europa`, `europaqual`, `conference`, `conferencequal`, `eflcup`, `efltrophy`, `facup`, and `wc26` outside June to August: `SPORT_ACTIVE_MONTHS['wc26']` is `[5,6,7]` with January as 0) `AmbientDO`'s baseline is 0: a viewer is the only caller, 180 requests per key per 3 h, each an ESPN scoreboard cache miss at 60 s against a 15 s TTL plus an uncached BSD call. That includes tonight's capture key and the 2026-11-17 proof (`efltrophy`). Corrected by review `wf_ae397945-0b8`, card F9: the first wording gave 25% for every key. That is the reason for 60 s and for the visibility gate. A relay-side `cf` cache on the BSD call would cut it and is relay-directed (Task 11).

## A load-dependent race in the render check, found by a later gate (2026-11-12)

`scripts/live-score-render-check.mjs` crashed in the unmutated baseline of mutation shard 2 of `verify-full` run 43170926697 (option1-chromium-gate at `4ce37c2`): `clock.pauseAt: Error: Cannot fast-forward to the past`, at `pg.clock.pauseAt(new Date(tNow + 1))` in the scenario that holds one request in flight and moves the clock only by `runFor`. The clock installed at `AT` runs until it is paused, so the page's time moves between reading `Date.now()` and the pause; a target 1 ms ahead is behind the page whenever that round trip takes more than a millisecond (measured: the `pauseAt` call took 1.3 to 2.9 ms idle and up to 17 ms under CPU load). ## Open questions for the owner (found by review `wf_584a08a5-824`, 2026-11-11; each is a design choice, none decided here)

1. **A reading has no age (poll S3 and S12).** The poll stops at kickoff + 3 h (the window CC proposed under your "auto-poll while a match is live" ruling, built as `LiveScore.liveWindowAfterStart`; you named the mode, not the numbers), and a hidden tab makes no request. A card keeps its last answer, `1–0`, `In progress`, `67'`, until the page is reloaded or the reader re-renders the slate (Today, the date buttons, Back); an open page never re-reads the archive, so a game the archive finalises meanwhile is not seen, and nothing on the card says how old the answer is. Real cases: a cup tie that runs to extra time and a shootout can pass 3 h from kickoff (the review estimated about 2 h 45 min or more; not measured), and a suspended match (lightning, routine in MLS) is still `In progress`. Options: (a) leave it as proposed; (b) extend the window for knockout competitions; (c) write the instant each answer was heard into the reading and have the card say `as of HH:mm` once it is older than two polls, and a named state when the window ends. (c) is the review's proposal and touches `LiveReading`, `liveNoteFor` and the render check; it has not been built.
2. **A re-render asks again at once (poll S0).** The Today button, the date buttons and Back re-run `start()`, which redraws the cards and starts a new poll whose first tick is immediate. A reader who presses Today every 20 s makes 3 requests per key per minute, and each new poll has its own one-request-per-key guard. Both skeptics rated it low. It is what makes a re-drawn card show its reading right away rather than a minute later. Options: (a) leave it, as built; (b) keep the last asked instant per key and day outside `startLivePoll`, and re-apply the last reading to the new cards. The conditions above say "one request per key per tick"; as built that holds inside one poll, and a re-render starts a new one.
